Scout Ultra Field Guide — Privacy policy

Privacy policy · updated 19 September 2026

What we collect: nothing. Scout Ultra Field Guide has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking. The app does not send your questions, your location, your notes or anything else to Scout Ultra or to anyone.

What stays on your phone. Your questions, recent asks, kit ticks, profile name and region, settings, and downloaded packs are stored on your device only, under your control. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location. If you allow it, the app reads your position to show it on the offline map and to compute bearing to a waypoint. It is used on the device and never transmitted by the app. You can revoke it in iOS Settings.

Microphone and speech. If you allow it, voice input uses on-device speech recognition; audio never leaves the phone. Read-aloud uses the on-device synthesiser.

Apple Intelligence. When on, the on-device model runs on your phone. Scout Ultra receives nothing.

Purchases. Full and region packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; we see no payment details. Purchase status is checked on your device against Apple's records.

Downloads. When you download a pack, your device connects to our pack server, which sees the ordinary connection data any web request carries (your IP address, the file requested, the time). We do not keep logs longer than needed to run the service and do not link them to you.

Sharing your location. The SHARE key on the map builds a message with your coordinates and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact. We will post changes here with a new date. Questions: adgentechteam@gmail.com.
