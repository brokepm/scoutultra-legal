Scout Ultra — Disclaimer

Not medical, legal or professional advice. Scout Ultra is a reference. It reprints published safety and first-aid guidance (American Heart Association, American Red Cross, CDC, FEMA/Ready.gov, USDA, EPA, NHTSA, CPSC, Wilderness Medical Society and others named in each guide's Sources) and answers written in advance from those sources. It is not a doctor, a paramedic, an electrician or a lawyer, and using it does not create a patient, client or professional relationship with anyone.

In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) first. The app's Call 911 switch dials your phone's dialler; the app itself cannot contact emergency services, does not know your medical history and cannot see what is in front of you.

Guidance changes and situations differ. Guides marked Reviewed were read by the named reviewer on the printed date; everything else is drafted and unreviewed, and says so. Guidance may be out of date, may not apply to you, and may be wrong for your situation. Use your own judgment and the judgment of any trained person present. Nothing here replaces first-aid training from an accredited provider.

Maps and position. Offline maps and your GPS position are approximate and may be out of date. Do not rely on them alone for navigation, hazard boundaries or evacuation routes; follow official instructions.

On-device AI. When Apple Intelligence is on, the app may point you to a step of a guide or, if you turn it on in Settings, write an answer from the stored sources. That text is generated by a machine, is marked as such, and can be wrong. The guide's own steps are never rewritten.
